About the National Cadet Corps

The youth wing of the Indian Armed Forces, grooming the youth of the country into disciplined and patriotic citizens.

The National Cadet Corps (NCC) is engaged in grooming the youth into disciplined and patriotic citizens. Comprising the Army, Navy, and Air Force wings, NCC provides exposure to military training, adventure sports, social service, and community development across schools and colleges in India.

Core Values

Unity & Discipline

Developing camaraderie, teamwork, and strict self-discipline among youth from diverse backgrounds.

Leadership Development

Instilling officer-like qualities, decision-making skills, and courage to lead from the front.

Selfless Service

Contributing actively to disaster relief, blood donation, tree plantation, and nation-building.

National Integration

Fostering pride in Indian heritage and strengthening unity across every state and culture.
