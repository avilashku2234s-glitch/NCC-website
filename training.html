Certification & Career Edge

NCC Certificate Progression

Earn nationally recognized certificates that offer bonus marks and direct SSB interview opportunities in the Indian Armed Forces.

A

Certificate 'A'

Junior Division / Wing (School Level)

Awarded to Class 8–10 cadets after completing 2 years of training and attending at least one Annual Training Camp (ATC).

C

Certificate 'C' (Highest)

Senior Division / Wing (College Level)

The highest NCC qualification. Holders with 'A' or 'B' grade get exemption from CDS/AFCAT written exams via NCC Special Entry for SSB.

B

Certificate 'B'

Senior Division / Wing (College Level)

Earned after 2 years in Senior Division/Wing with 75% parade attendance and one camp. Serves as the mandatory gateway for the 'C' Certificate.
