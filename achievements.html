HIGHEST HONOUR
Kartavya Path Glory

Republic Day Parade & Prime Minister's Banner

Every year on 26th January, the finest NCC marching contingents from all 17 Directorates salute the President of India at Kartavya Path, New Delhi. The best performing Directorate is awarded the prestigious Prime Minister's Banner during the PM's Rally.

Milestones of Excellence

Key Accomplishments Across Domains

Filter through NCC's landmark achievements in international diplomacy, adventure, sports, and social service.

Adventure

Conquering Mount Everest

NCC boys and girls mountaineering teams successfully scaled Mount Everest (8,848.86m) in 2013, 2016, and 2025—setting national youth mountaineering records.

International

Youth Exchange Programme (YEP)

Over 150+ top NCC cadets represent India every year as Youth Ambassadors in friendly countries including the UK, Russia, Singapore, Vietnam, and Kazakhstan.

Environment

Puneet Sagar Abhiyan

A nationwide mega-campaign where lakhs of cadets cleaned beaches, riverbanks, and lakes—collecting and recycling hundreds of tonnes of plastic waste.

Sports & Shooting

GV Mavalankar & National Shooting

NCC shooters regularly clinch gold and silver medals at All India GV Mavalankar Shooting Championships and represent India in international shooting events.

National Service

Ex-NCC Yogdan & Disaster Relief

During floods, cyclones, and national emergencies, trained Senior Division cadets assist civil administration in crowd control, relief distribution, and medical aid.

Defence Entry

Officers in Indian Armed Forces

Thousands of 'C' Certificate holders join the Indian Military Academy (IMA), OTA, Indian Naval Academy, and Air Force Academy every year as commissioned officers.

Legacy of Leadership

Distinguished Alumni Who Wore the NCC Uniform

From the highest constitutional offices to Param Vir Chakra awardees and space heroes, NCC alumni have led India across every sphere.
