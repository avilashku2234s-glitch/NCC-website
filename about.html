About National Cadet Corps

The largest uniformed youth organization in the world—moulding young minds into disciplined, resilient, and patriotic leaders for tomorrow's India.

OUR HERITAGE & GENESIS

Shaping India's Youth Since 1948

The National Cadet Corps (NCC) came into existence on 16 July 1948 under the XXXI Act of Parliament, based on the recommendations of the H.N. Kunzru Committee. Its roots trace back to the University Corps created under the Indian Defence Act of 1917.

Headquartered in New Delhi, NCC operates under the Ministry of Defence and is open to school and college students on a voluntary basis. Cadets are given basic military training in small arms and parades, instilling values of secularism, selfless service, and national integration.

1948

NCC officially established under the NCC Act.

1949

Girls Division raised to empower young women.

1950 & 1952

Air Wing (1950) and Naval Wing (1952) added.

1965 & 1971

Cadets served as the second line of defence in wars.

TRI-SERVICE STRUCTURE

The Three Wings of NCC

Represented by the three colors of the NCC flag—Red for Army, Deep Blue for Navy, and Light Blue for Air Force—cadets can choose their preferred service wing.

Red Stripe

Army Wing

The largest wing of the NCC, focusing on ground combat fundamentals, infantry tactics, physical endurance, and fieldcraft.

  • Weapon Training & Firing (.22 Rifle)
  • Map Reading, Field Craft & Battle Craft
  • Thal Sainik Camp (TSC) & RDC Parade
  • Trekking, Mountaineering & Obstacle Courses
Deep Blue Stripe

Naval Wing

Designed to impart maritime awareness, seamanship skills, and naval traditions to cadets across coastal and inland units.

  • Boat Pulling, Sailing & Whaler Rigging
  • Naval Navigation, Semaphore & Signaling
  • Nau Sainik Camp (NSC) & Ship Attachment
  • Scuba Diving, Swimming & Ship Modeling
Light Blue Stripe

Air Wing

Introduces cadets to aviation, aerodynamics, and air force operations, inspiring future pilots and aerospace engineers.

  • Microlight Flying & Gliding Experience
  • Aero-Modeling (Static & Radio-Controlled)
  • Vayu Sainik Camp (VSC) & Air Base Visits
  • Air Navigation, Meteorology & Principles of Flight
OUR MISSION

Core Aims of the NCC

Character & Leadership

To develop character, comradeship, discipline, a secular outlook, the spirit of adventure, and ideals of selfless service amongst young citizens.

Organized & Trained Youth

To create a human resource of organized, trained, and motivated youth to provide leadership in all walks of life and always be available for the service of the nation.

Armed Forces Motivation

To provide a suitable environment to motivate the youth to take up a career in the Indian Armed Forces (Army, Navy, and Air Force).
